Surface material classification from visual and haptic data is important for teleoperation and robotic recognition, but real-world datasets are often class-imbalanced. Existing classifiers tend to favor majority classes and neglect minority ones, degrading overall performance. This work formulates the classification problem within a double deep Q-network (Double-DQN) framework to mitigate the effect of unbalanced visual and haptic data.
