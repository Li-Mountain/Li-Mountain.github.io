This review surveys 146 case studies from 2020 to 2024 to examine how computing technologies, particularly emerging ones, are transforming cultural heritage preservation. The digital cultural heritage process (DCHP) is analyzed across five stages: acquisition, archiving, restoration, analysis and valuation, and presentation and promotion. The review further discusses the integration of interdisciplinary computing methods and multimodal data, and identifies open challenges including sustainability, data reuse, and ethics.
