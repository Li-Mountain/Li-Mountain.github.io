This letter studies force-tactile signals produced by fingertips sliding on physical surfaces, which tactile-communication applications deliver to surface-haptics devices for texture display. To meet reliability and latency requirements, we compress the signals into compact representations before transmission and approximately reconstruct them before display. The method combines a stacked auto-encoder (SAE) for compression and reconstruction with a gated recurrent unit (GRU) that suppresses channel noise on the received representations. Evaluations on the HapTex and TUM184 datasets show a high compression ratio with small reconstruction error, efficient online implementation, and no reliance on statistical assumptions about the signals or channel noise.
