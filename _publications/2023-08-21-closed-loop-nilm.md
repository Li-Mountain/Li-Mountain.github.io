Nonintrusive load monitoring (NILM) estimates the power of every load in a smart home from only the aggregate power measured at the main bus, but its accuracy degrades for loads with more than two operating states. We propose a closed-loop learning method that introduces a feedback mechanism to reduce NILM estimation error. A feedforward network disaggregates the aggregate power into individual load powers, while a feedback network balances operating states and exploits temporal-spatial correlations among loads to produce a feedback loss that refines the feedforward network. Results show improved NILM accuracy and suitability for practical deployment.
